# Lore outline

Placeholder; the story is written in roadmap phase 11 (P11.1). Names and beats below are working titles. Terms follow the [glossary](DESIGN.md#glossary). This text is AI-drafted and tracked in [CONTENT-PROVENANCE](CONTENT-PROVENANCE.md).

## Premise

A survey ship carrying a sealed biological payload breaks up over an uncharted world. The payload, an engineered organism meant to terraform, was kept in the ship's lab; in the crash it escaped and has already spread on its own: **the Strain**. Where it took root it formed **nidi**, living growths that push it outward through soil, water and every creature it reaches. You are the only survivor at the crash site.

## Arc

1. **Crash site:** salvage, shelter, first contact with hosts, the Strain-infected wildlife. Logs from the ship's lab hint the Strain was made, not found.
2. **The first nidus:** follow the festering land to a nidus and learn that destroying it halts the spread but does not heal the ground.
3. **Research:** host tissue, nidus cores and festered soil reveal how the Strain rewrites living things and land, and that its own miasma can be processed into tools against it.
4. **Regions:** each region adds a twist (resistant variants, surges, infected machinery), unlocking new tech and pushing the frontier back one remission zone at a time.
5. **The Counterstrain:** from unstable prototypes, engineer an organism that spreads like the Strain but heals. The Strain adapts with new variants; you defend the final deployment.
6. **Remission:** the world heals visibly. The name of the mod is the ending.
