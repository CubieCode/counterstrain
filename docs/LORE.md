# Lore outline

Placeholder; the story is written in roadmap phase 10. Names and beats below are working titles.

## Premise

A survey ship carrying a sealed biological payload breaks up over an uncharted world. The payload, an engineered organism meant to terraform, has already escaped and spread on its own: the **blight**. You are the only survivor at the crash site.

## Arc

1. **Crash site:** salvage, shelter, first contact with corrupted wildlife. Logs from the ship hint the blight was made, not found.
2. **The first heart:** follow the spread to a heart, learn that destroying it halts but does not heal.
3. **Research:** samples and scans reveal how the blight rewrites living things and soil.
4. **Regions:** each region adds a twist (adaptive strains, surges, corrupted machines), unlocking new tech.
5. **Counter-strain:** engineer an organism that spreads like the blight but heals. The blight adapts; you defend the final deployment.
6. **Remission:** the world heals visibly. The name of the mod is the ending.

## Open questions

Who built the payload and why; whether other survivors exist; how much of the story lives in quest text versus found items (logs, data items, structures).
