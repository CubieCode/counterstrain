# Remission changelog

Versions follow `mod_version` in `gradle.properties`; tags are `mod-v<version>`.

## Unreleased

- Project scaffold (P0.1).
- Entity corruption state: the `remission:corruption` attachment (Strain level 0–100 and host stage), saved with the entity and synced to tracking clients, plus the common config `hosts.stageThresholds` (P2.1).
- Debug commands `/remission infect <targets> <level>`, `/remission cleanse <targets>` and `/remission inspect <target>` (permission level 2) to set, clear and read host state (P2.3).
- Scanner (`remission:scanner`): use it on a host for a host scan or on a block for a block scan, data items whose `remission:scan_data` component records the entity type and host stage or the block (P6.1). Placeholder textures.
- Bestiary (P6.2): every living, non-immune entity type of every installed mod is a host type; host scans record the highest host stage scanned per type for each player (`remission:bestiary` player attachment, kept on death; updates sync to that player, and clients tracking the player also get it on first sight); scans of types the bestiary does not list record nothing; the key M ("Open Bestiary") shows the list and "X of N host types scanned".
- Hidden bestiary types (P6.3): entity type tag `#remission:bestiary_hidden` (giant, illusioner and armour stand by default) keeps types players cannot normally meet as hosts out of the bestiary list and total; packs extend or replace it.
- Festering land groundwork (P4.1): the `remission:nidus` block (placeholder texture), a per-chunk Strain level (`remission:chunk_corruption` attachment) and a tick-budgeted spread engine that festers chunks outward from each nidus and catches unloaded land up when it loads, with a `[spread]` common config section for rates and budgets. Breaking a nidus stops its spread.
- Public API (P4.4): `com.cubiecode.remission.api` posts host corrupted and cleansed, chunk stage changed, nidus destroyed and cancellable Strain spread attempt events, and `CorruptionQueries` answers stage and host queries.
- Live spread waits for catch-up (P4.5): a reloaded chunk still queued for catch-up is skipped by live passes until it is caught up, so it no longer loses the time it was away when a pass comes due first.
- Cave festering (P4.6): from festering stage 3, blocks with a `remission:festers_into` entry that face air (cave walls, floors and ceilings, overhangs) fester too, at any height: completely down to Y=0 and one block in four below it, picked by position. Rock inside the ground and ores stay. Runs after the surface pass on its own small per-tick budget and resumes after an unload. New block `remission:festered_deepslate` (placeholder texture); new optional `underground_stage` field on `festers_into` entries (default 3, `0` turns cave festering off for that entry). Default stone entries changed: `#minecraft:stone_ore_replaceables` festers into festered stone and `#minecraft:deepslate_ore_replaceables` (deepslate, tuff) into festered deepslate, replacing the single `#minecraft:base_stone_overworld` entry. A chunk that dropped below full status while its festering was queued is now picked up again when it returns.
- Festering stages (P4.2): festering land now shows. As a chunk's festering stage rises, grass turns to `remission:festering_grass` (stage 1), logs and leaves to `remission:festering_log` and `remission:festering_leaves` (stage 2) and surface stone to `remission:festered_stone` (stage 3), a few columns per tick; at the last stage the chunk's biome flips to the new `remission:fester` biome and players see the new colours at once. Data maps `remission:festers_into` (which blocks fester into what, and at which stage) and `remission:fester_variant` (a per-source-biome fester biome) let packs cover modded blocks and biomes. New API event `BlockFesteredEvent`. Placeholder textures.
- Infection: a mob killed by a host becomes a host (or is replaced by its profile's `convert_to` type); `#remission:immune` (bosses by default) never become hosts; players are never infected on kill; datapack registry `remission:mob_profile` (attributes, behaviour, `convert_to`, conversion flags, priority; world reload to apply); loot condition `remission:is_corrupted`; loot modifier codec `remission:add_item` and the default `remission:host_samples`, which adds the new `remission:host_tissue` item to host death loot (P3.3).
- Host fallbacks (P3.2): flying hosts (bats, parrots, bees, modded flyers) and swimming hosts (fish, squid, dolphins) chase and bite prey through the air or water, while flying and swimming monsters (ghasts, phantoms, guardians) turn their own attacks on Remission's targets; brain-based hosts that can fight (piglins, hoglins, axolotls, zoglins, wardens; breezes against players and iron golems) get their brain's attack target fed with prey, and are angered at it where their brain needs that. Remission's first default mob profile, `remission:unsupported`, marks villagers, allays and frogs `none` (they become hosts but keep their own AI).
- Quest gates (P8.1): advancement triggers `remission:nidus_destroyed` (a player breaks a nidus; `location`), `remission:host_scanned` (`entity`, `stage`, `bestiary_types`) and `remission:block_scanned` (`location`), and custom stats `remission:nidi_destroyed`, `remission:hosts_scanned` and `remission:blocks_scanned` on the statistics screen, for FTB Quests or any quest mod. Remission ships no advancements; packs define them.
- Miasma and samples (P7.1): the `remission:miasma` fluid (source and flowing, liquid block, `remission:miasma_bucket`); with Mekanism installed, a `remission:miasma` chemical (optional integration, nothing changes without Mekanism); sample items `remission:nidus_core` (a broken nidus now drops one) and `remission:festered_soil` (a 25% chance from dirt, grass and other `#remission:festered_soil_sources` blocks broken in chunks at festering stage 2 or higher, via the new `remission:is_festering` loot condition and the `remission:festered_soil_samples` modifier). Placeholder textures.
- Festering land infects mobs (P4.7): a mob that stays in festering land at festering stage 2 or higher, on the surface or underground, becomes a host after 5 minutes (or turns into its profile's `convert_to` type); time spent elsewhere wears the exposure off, and it is saved with the mob. Immune mobs and mobs whose profile sets `conversion.festering_land` to false are spared. New `[infection]` common config section (`festeringLand`, `festeringLandMinStage`, `festeringLandExposureTicks`) and API cause `CorruptionCause.FESTERING_LAND`.
- Festering veins and contact infection (P4.8): new block `remission:festering_vein` (placeholder texture) creeps over the solid faces of blocks in chunks at festering stage 2 or higher, on the surface, in caves and over player builds, without replacing anything; more stages cover more faces, festering chunks are revisited about once a minute so new walls get overgrown too, and veins drop only with silk touch. Block tag `#remission:vein_resistant` (empty) keeps veins off chosen blocks. Mobs touching a block in the new tag `#remission:infectious` (festering grass, the festered blocks and veins) or swimming in water in festering land become hosts after 5 seconds of contact (or turn into their profile's `convert_to` type); players never do. New config `[festering] veins`, `veinMinStage` and `[infection] contact`, `contactTicks`, and API cause `CorruptionCause.CONTACT`; veins post `BlockFesteredEvent`.
- Surges (P4.3): each dimension gets a surge from a nidus about once an in-game day once a player is in its reach (config `[surges]`), or on `/remission surge start [pos] [intensity]` (`stop`, `status`). A title, the raid horn and a boss bar warn players; then the nidus spreads faster, hosts from `#remission:surge_hosts` rise near players on festering land and in caves beneath it (respecting each type's spawn rules under cover, none on Peaceful, capped per surge), and drifting `remission:miasma_cloud` clouds are released above festering land. `SurgeStartedEvent` and `SurgeEndedEvent` in the public API, `CorruptionCause.SURGE`, the `remission:surge_survived` trigger (`intensity`) and the `remission:surges_survived` stat. Placeholder cloud texture.
- Viral load (P5.1): players build up viral load (0–100) in festering land, by festering stage and less the higher they fly above the ground (in full on the ground, underground and under any roof), plus more in water there, from water bottles drunk there and inside miasma clouds; it wears off elsewhere. Rising viral load brings hunger, weakness, mining fatigue and slowness, and at 100 damage ("succumbed to the Strain", damage type `remission:viral_load`); death resets it. A HUD meter above the hunger bar (client option `[hud] showViralLoad`; placeholder icon), the `[viral_load]` common config section, `ViralLoadChangedEvent` and `CorruptionQueries.viralLoad` in the public API, and the `remission:viral_load_changed` trigger (`viral_load`). Network protocol version 2.
- Purification (P5.2): the handheld purifier `remission:handheld_purifier` cleanses festered blocks and veins around it and lowers its chunk's Strain level, or cleanses a host; the `remission:quarantine_pylon` wards a square of chunks (`[purification] pylonRadius`), where spread, festering, veins and contact infection stop, festered blocks are cleansed and the land drains; plantable `remission:mendroot` slowly cleanses around it. A chunk purified to zero enters remission, with `[remission_zones]` bonuses (no natural monster spawns, crop growth and yield; `#remission:remission_yields`). Touching festered blocks or veins adds viral load (`[viral_load] contact`, `contactGain`). Data map `remission:cleanses_into`, API events `BlockPurifiedEvent` and `ChunkEnteredRemissionEvent`, `PurificationCause`, `CorruptionCause.PURIFICATION` and `CorruptionQueries.isInRemission`; `ChunkStageChangedEvent` now also fires when stages fall. Trigger `remission:region_cleansed` (`radius`, `share`, `chunks`) and stat `remission:chunks_cleansed`. Placeholder textures.
- Machines (P7.2): the analyzer, sequencer and synthesizer (crafted from vanilla parts), with recipe types `remission:analyzing`, `remission:sequencing` and `remission:synthesizing` (item and fluid ingredients, item and fluid results, energy and time; datapack-editable). Each has input slots, two output slots, input and output tanks and an FE buffer, and exposes standard item, fluid and energy capabilities, so pipes and storage networks can run it. Defaults: the analyzer turns samples into miasma (and festered soil and nidus cores into block scans), the sequencer turns a scan and host tissue in miasma into the new `remission:strain_sequence`, and the synthesizer cultures host tissue from a strain sequence (a placeholder until serums). Recipe categories appear in JEI and in EMI; neither is required. Placeholder textures and screen art.
- Team query (P4.12): `TeamQueries.areTeammates` answers "are these two players teammates?" by UUID (either player may be offline) for every rule that involves teammates, through the new `TeamLookupEvent` that packs and integrations answer; with nothing answering, each player is their own team. With FTB Teams installed (optional), members of one FTB team are teammates. Answers are cached for 5 seconds and follow FTB Teams changes at once.
- Revenants (P5.3): a player who dies to the Strain (killed by a host or revenant, by viral load, or in festering land) rises as a `remission:revenant` with their skin, armour and weapon, holding exactly the items (Curios slots included) and experience the death would have dropped; other deaths and `keepInventory` are vanilla. It stays near where they fell, never despawns, is hurt only by players and `/kill`, and returns from the void. When slain, its items can be picked up only by the owner and the owner's teammates (the team query) until `[revenants] pickupTimeoutTicks` frees them. New API events `RevenantRisenEvent` and `RevenantSlainEvent`, query `CorruptionQueries.isRevenant`, cause `CorruptionCause.REVENANT`, trigger `remission:revenant_reclaimed` and stat `remission:revenants_reclaimed`; a saved per-dimension revenant index; optional Curios support.
- Quest-fired events and map overlay (P8.2): `/remission nidus seed <pos>` plants a nidus and `/remission region advance <pos> <radius> [stages]` makes the Strain advance by whole festering stages through the loaded chunks around a position (permission level 2), so quests (FTB Quests command rewards) and scripts can start a new source, advance a region, or start a surge with `/remission surge start`. With JourneyMap installed (optional, client side), festering chunks show on its full-screen map, minimap and web map, shaded by stage from pale ochre to violet, and remission zones in pale cyan, for every chunk seen this session. Network protocol version 3 (new payload `remission:festering_map`).
- Countermeasures (P5.7): Strain hazards (plain ids; `remission:contact`, touching festered blocks, is the first) and countermeasures that cure them for good. The synthesizer makes a `remission:countermeasure_protocol` (placeholder texture) from a strain sequence, festered soil and paper in miasma; using it protects the user and their team (online teammates at once, the rest when they log in or join). `StrainHazards` answers whether a hazard applies to a player or, for block hazards, a placed block (only while the placer's team is online by default, `[countermeasures] blockHazards`), with a cancellable `StrainHazardEvent` so packs can hold a hazard back. Also `CountermeasureGrantedEvent`, `/remission countermeasure grant|revoke|list`, trigger `remission:countermeasure_gained` and stat `remission:countermeasures_gained`.
- Festering land atmosphere (P4.11): the `remission:fester` biome has its own ambient loop, mood sounds, wet, organic additions and music (placeholder sounds), all unlike the Deep Dark, and the fog thickens with the festering stage of the chunk you stand in, from a light haze to thick mauve fog at the last stage. The new client option `atmosphere.festerFog` (0 to 1) turns it down or off. Fester variant biomes inherit none of the biome's effects; the remission-data-formats skill lists what to copy.
